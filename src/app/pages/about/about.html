About Gaya-Store

Clothes made to be lived in.

Gaya-Store began with a simple idea: a wardrobe should be built from fewer, better pieces. We make modern essentials with honest fabrics and quiet details — clothes you reach for again and again.

Our story

From a small studio to your everyday wardrobe.

What started as a handful of pieces sewn in a small studio has grown into a collection worn across the country. Our approach hasn't changed: design slowly, choose materials carefully, and make every garment earn its place.

Each collection is built around versatile silhouettes and a neutral palette, so everything you buy works with what you already own.
